How We Handle Your Information

Plain-language answers to what we collect, why, and how you can ask us to change or delete it.

Last updated: [DD Month YYYY]

SIDDHI ElderCare Services ("SIDDHI," "we," "us," or "our") provides non-medical elder-care coordination services in and around Mumbai and Pune, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you visit our website, submit our contact form, or communicate with our care coordinators by phone or WhatsApp.

By using this website or contacting us, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not submit your information to us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us, mainly through the "Send Us A Message" form on our Contact page, and through direct phone or WhatsApp conversations with our coordinators. This may include:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your city or general location
  • Details you share about your parent's or family member's care needs, health situation, or living arrangements
  • Any messages, call recordings, or WhatsApp conversations you have with our team

We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies on this website. If that changes, this policy will be updated and, where required, you will be shown a cookie notice.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and get back to you about care options
  • To coordinate and deliver the care services you or your family engage us for
  • To send appointment reminders, well-being check updates, and service-related WhatsApp or phone messages
  • To improve our services and website
  • To meet legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

3. WhatsApp and Phone Communications

If you share your phone number with us, our coordinators may contact you by phone call or WhatsApp about your inquiry or ongoing care plan, including the periodic family well-being updates described on our site. You can ask to stop receiving non-essential WhatsApp updates at any time by telling your coordinator or messaging us directly.

4. Sharing Of Information

We share personal information only where necessary to provide our services:

  • With our own care coordinators and caregiving staff directly involved in your case
  • With hospitals, clinics, or emergency services, where you have asked us to coordinate medical or emergency support
  • With service providers who help us run this website and process inquiries (for example, our website hosting provider and the form-processing service used to deliver contact-form submissions to our team)
  • Where required by law, court order, or to protect someone's safety in an emergency

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

Our website is hosted on Vercel, with website content stored in a private GitHub repository. Our admin area is protected by a password-based login and a signed, encrypted session cookie. While we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We keep contact-form inquiries and communication records for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, deliver services, and meet our legal and accounting obligations, after which we delete or anonymize them.

7. Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Ask us to delete your personal information, subject to any legal obligation we may have to retain it
  • Withdraw any consent you previously gave us, such as for WhatsApp updates
  • Raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (below), or with the relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9.

8. Children's and Elder Information

Our services are intended for adults arranging care on behalf of themselves or a family member. Where you share information about an elderly parent or relative on their behalf, you confirm you are authorized to do so and that it is being shared to help us provide care to them.

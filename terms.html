1. About Our Services

SIDDHI ElderCare Services provides non-medical home care coordination for seniors and adults, including well-being checks, hospital and appointment coordination, emergency-response coordination, logistics support, and related paperwork or billing assistance, as described on our Services page.

Our Services are delivered on the ground in Mumbai and nearby areas, including Navi Mumbai, Thane, and Virar (see our Contact page for current service areas).

We coordinate and facilitate access to care, hospitals, and support networks. We are not a hospital, clinic, ambulance service, or licensed medical practice, and nothing on this Site or provided by our coordinators is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

In a medical emergency, contact local emergency services first. In India, dial 112 (national emergency number) or 108 (ambulance), or go to the nearest hospital, and then notify us. If you are abroad, call our coordinators immediately on the numbers on our Contact page and, if you can, ask someone near your parent to call 108 or 112 straight away.

3. Arranging Care From Abroad

If you live outside India and arrange care for a parent or relative in India, the following applies:

  • Authority and awareness. You confirm that you are authorized to arrange care for the person receiving it and that, where they are able to understand, they know about and agree to the arrangement.
  • Contacts. Please name one primary contact (usually you) and, where possible, a backup contact in India, such as a relative, friend, or neighbour, whom we can reach if you are unreachable because of time zones or travel. We act on instructions from your named primary contact and may ask to verify identity before acting on instructions given on your parent's behalf.
  • Emergencies. In an emergency we will act first to get your parent to medical help, for example by calling an ambulance or taking them to the nearest hospital, and will inform you as soon as we can. We do not wait for prior approval in that situation.
  • Medical decisions. Treatment decisions, consent, and hospital admissions remain with your parent, their doctors, and, where needed, their legally authorized representative. We coordinate care but cannot give consent to treatment on anyone's behalf.
  • Updates. We share updates by WhatsApp or phone. These are summaries from our coordinators, not medical reports.
  • Time zones. We aim to respond within a reasonable time, but where something is urgent we may contact you at a time that is inconvenient in your time zone.

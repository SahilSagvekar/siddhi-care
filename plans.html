Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a plan be customised to our family's needs?

Yes. Every plan is a starting point — your coordinator will adjust visit frequency, add-ons, and check-in schedules once they understand your parents' routine and needs.

What exactly is covered in the plan price?

Your coordinator's visits, on-call support, and coordination work are included. Third-party costs — like doctor fees, hospital bills, or medicines — are billed separately and always shared with you in advance.

Can I subscribe to only part of a plan?

In most cases, yes — talk to a care advisor about which parts of a plan matter most to your family, and we'll put together something that fits.

Do you provide care for parents with dementia?

Yes, with attendants trained specifically for cognitive care and a plan built around your family's situation.

Is there a lock-in period?

No. You can pause, switch plans, or cancel at any time — we don't believe in locking families into a plan that no longer fits.

Can I upgrade my plan later if my parents' needs change?

Absolutely — your coordinator will flag when a change in health or routine means a different plan would serve your parents better, and upgrading takes effect immediately.

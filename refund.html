Cancellations And Refunds

How changes, cancellations, and refunds work for paid SIDDHI ElderCare Services plans.

Last updated: [DD Month YYYY]

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for paid care plans and services booked through SIDDHI ElderCare Services ("SIDDHI," "we," "us," or "our"). It applies alongside our Terms & Conditions.

1. Cancelling A Service Plan

You may cancel an ongoing care plan at any time by notifying your care coordinator or writing to us at support@siddhieldercare.com. Cancellations take effect after a notice period of [X days], so we can safely hand off or wind down any ongoing coordination, appointments, or caregiver visits.

2. Refund Eligibility

  • If you cancel before your plan's service period has started, you will receive a full refund of any advance payment, minus any non-refundable onboarding or assessment fee disclosed to you at the time of booking.
  • If you cancel partway through a paid period (for example, a monthly plan), we will refund the unused, prorated portion of that period, minus the notice-period charge described in Section 1.
  • We do not refund amounts for services, visits, or coordination work already completed at the time of cancellation.

3. Non-Refundable Items

The following are non-refundable once incurred, unless otherwise agreed in writing:

  • [Registration / onboarding / assessment fee, if any]
  • Third-party costs already paid on your behalf (for example, ambulance, hospital, or equipment charges we coordinated at your request)

4. How Refunds Are Processed

To request a refund, contact us with your name, the plan or invoice reference, and the reason for cancellation. Approved refunds are processed within [X business days] of approval, back to the original payment method used, unless we mutually agree on an alternative.

5. Cancellations Initiated By Us

In the rare case that we are unable to continue providing a service — for example, if we cannot safely staff a case — we will notify you as early as possible and refund any amount paid for care that was not delivered.

6. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. The terms in place at the time you booked a plan govern that booking, unless a change is required by law.

7. Contact

For any cancellation or refund request, reach us at support@siddhieldercare.com.
